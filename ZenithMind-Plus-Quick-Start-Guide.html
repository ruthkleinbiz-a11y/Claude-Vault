The Path

Page 4

You have 8 skills. They all work independently, but they're dramatically more powerful in this order. Each one builds on what came before it — your positioning sharpens your story, your story sharpens your credentials, your credentials sharpen everything you put in front of other people.

Our recommendation: Run skills 1 through 5 in order — they build your complete professional foundation. Skills 6 through 8 are situational tools you run whenever you need them.

Build Your Foundation (run these in order)
1
Unique Genius Discovery
Cross-references 20+ frameworks from your Scorecard to find the thing only you can do. Produces a positioning statement for your website, pitch deck, and bios. Start here — everything else builds on this.
2
Story Sharpener
Takes your positioning and writes every version of your story — 50-word bio, about page, origin story, keynote opening, elevator pitch. Every place your story needs to appear, it's already written.
3
Credential Forge
Finds the authority you already have and packages it for whatever room you're walking into. Six deployment-ready angles for conferences, boards, podcasts, and pitches.
4
First Impression Fix
Audits your LinkedIn, bio, and pitch against who you actually are — then rewrites everything using your positioning, story, and credentials from steps 1-3.
5
Signature Scribe
Builds a Voice Calibration Map — how you argue, open, and close — then writes any of 13 document types in your voice: keynotes, proposals, executive summaries, and more.
Use Anytime (run these whenever you need them)
6
Scouting Report
Run before any high-stakes conversation. Tells you what to lead with, where you'll trip up, and how to recover.
7
Network Playbook
Names who actually decides your next career move and gives you the exact approach for each one.
8
Reality Check
Run when you feel misread. Exposes the gap between how your industry sees you and what's actually true.

You can run any skill at any time — the path above is the recommended order, not a requirement. But if you follow it, each skill's output makes the next one sharper. The foundation you build in steps 1-5 makes the situational tools dramatically more effective.

How It Works

Each skill produces a professional PDF report saved to your ZenithMind folder on your computer. Your data stays local — not in the cloud. Run a skill as many times as you want — each run can target a different situation, audience, or context.
