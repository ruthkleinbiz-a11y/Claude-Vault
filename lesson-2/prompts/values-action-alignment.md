## What It Does

Exposes the incongruence between the user's STATED values and their ACTUAL behavioral patterns. The core premise: most people live by values they inherited, not values they chose — and the gap between stated and lived values is a primary driver of the dissatisfaction and self-sabotage revealed by the Mirror Prompt.

"Ghost values" concept from source: values the user claims to hold but which were inherited from family, culture, or social pressure rather than authentically chosen. These ghost values show up as the stated value but don't generate the behavior associated with living that value.
