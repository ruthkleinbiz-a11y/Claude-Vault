## What It Does

Diagnoses and transforms harmful comparison patterns. Turns the energy of comparison (which drains) into a self-reference framework (which fuels). Rich's framing: "Stops you from comparing yourself to others — it's draining, and it kills your progress — this prompt helps you focus on your own path."

**Design note from source:** Uses "MANDATORY OPERATIONAL DIRECTIVES" — Claude leads with analysis first, not questions. This is the opposite of most other protocols in the system. Claude does not start by asking the user questions — it generates the analysis of their comparison patterns first, then invites dialogue.
