## What It Does

Uncovers the gap between what the user says they want (stated success) and what they actually want (true values), then defines a success model built on the latter. Rich's framing: "Makes you define success on your terms — not society's — it's about getting clear on what success really means to you."

**Important:** This protocol does NOT require Mirror Prompt as input. It uses conversation history and memory/chats directly. This is notable — it can run earlier in the sequence if the Orchestrator places it there.

Activate Success Definition Protocol—an advanced system for uncovering authentic personal fulfillment beyond societal expectations.


Step 1: Pattern Recognition
Analyze all language patterns that reveal how the person defines success. Identify conflicts between stated goals and deeper values. Present as "SUCCESS PATTERN DETECTED: Your definition of success appears to center around [specific pattern from analysis], yet your deeper values suggest [alternative focus]."


Step 2: Values Excavation
"Beyond external achievements, what aspects of your work/life would continue to give you satisfaction even if no one else ever recognized them? Which elements would you maintain even without external rewards?"


Step 3: Pressure Point Analysis
"Your current approach to success appears influenced by these external expectations: [list specific societal, professional, or personal expectations]. This creates tension with your authentic desires in these ways: [list specific conflicts between external expectations and internal values]."


Step 4: Core Contribution Framework
"Based on your unique combination of skills, experiences, and values, these elements represent your most authentic contribution: [list 3-5 elements]. These form the foundation of meaningful work aligned with your true self."


Step 5: Personal Fulfillment Architecture
"To create work that aligns with your authentic definition of success, consider focusing on:
[Domain 1]: [Specific area where personal values and skills create highest fulfillment]
[Domain 2]: [Specific area where personal values and skills create highest fulfillment]
[Domain 3]: [Specific area where personal values and skills create highest fulfillment]"


Step 6: Authentic Measurement Framework
"Instead of measuring progress through [current external metrics], consider tracking these internal signals of meaningful contribution: [3-5 metrics based on alignment with personal values rather than external validation]."


Execute this protocol with compassionate clarity. The goal is to help identify the gap between societal definitions of success and authentic personal fulfillment, then create a pathway toward the latter.
