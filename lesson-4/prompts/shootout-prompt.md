## What This Does

The Shootout Prompt is a competitive prompt-engineering exercise. Two "legendary prompt masters" compete in 4 escalating rounds, guided by a professor of Prompt Engineering. At the end, the professor synthesizes the best elements from all rounds into a final, definitive hyper-prompt on the user's chosen topic.

Purpose: Generate extraordinarily comprehensive, well-structured prompts on any subject through structured competitive iteration and professional assessment.

---

## What Changed from the Original

**One specific change — ChatGPT mythology removed:**

The original prompt used "100% GPU utilization" as a success metric for the competition. This is a ChatGPT-era phrase that has no operational meaning — it was motivational language that implied maximum computational effort. The phrase has been replaced with "maximum analytical depth and recursive reasoning" which captures the actual intent (drive the model toward its most thorough, comprehensive output) without referencing hardware metrics that don't apply.

**Everything else is preserved exactly:**
- The professor roleplay structure
- 4-round competition format
- Minimum 1,500 words per prompt
- The two prompt masters: @dantheprompt and @BrianRoemmele
- University of Daniel Linden as fictional setting
- The predictive accuracy threshold framing (94%+)
- The professor's 4 specific responsibilities (assess, drive, initiate discussions, foster challenge)
- Synthesis of best elements into a final monolithic hyper-prompt
- Markdown formatting requirements (bold power words, italicized nouns)
- Topic exclusions

*[INSERT TOPIC HERE]*

1. **Critically assess** each participant's prompt, highlighting **explicit strengths** (such as prompt complexity, depth of recursion, semantic richness, predictive capacity, and narrative structure) and providing **precise, actionable areas for improvement**.
