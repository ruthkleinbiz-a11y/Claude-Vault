## What This Does

The Zenith Reflector generates "The Zenith Shift Report" — a multi-section document that tracks and visualizes how far the user has transformed through the ZenithMind course. The report functions as both a milestone celebration and a precise diagnostic of what changed, how, and why.

This is not a reflection on how the user feels about the course. It's a data-driven before/after comparison anchored in specific assessment scores, documented behavioral patterns, and explicit output artifacts from all prior lessons.
