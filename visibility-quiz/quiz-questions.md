## The 8 Quiz Questions

---

**Question 1: When someone asks what you do, what happens?**

A) I give a long explanation — and I can tell I'm losing them halfway through.
B) I have a decent answer, but it doesn't feel like it sticks with people.
C) I have a clear pitch, but I'm not consistently in front of the right people to use it.
D) I have a sharp, memorable answer that makes people immediately want to know more.

---

**Question 2: How would you describe your current online presence?**

A) Minimal — I know I need to do something, but I haven't figured out where to start.
B) Inconsistent — I post when I have time, but there's no real strategy behind it.
C) Active — I show up regularly, but I'm not sure it's translating into real opportunities.
D) Strategic — my presence is intentional, consistent, and generates leads and referrals.

---

**Question 3: When it comes to your expertise and credibility, which feels most true?**

A) I have deep expertise, but almost no one outside my immediate circle knows it.
B) People who know me see my value — but I struggle to translate that to strangers.
C) I'm building my authority and getting some recognition, but I want to accelerate it.
D) I'm seen as a go-to expert in my field — people seek me out, not the other way around.

---

**Question 4: How do most of your new clients or opportunities come to you?**

A) Almost entirely by accident — a referral here, a connection there. Nothing predictable.
B) Word of mouth mostly, but it's unpredictable and I can't control the volume.
C) A mix of referrals and some intentional outreach — I'm starting to create flow, but it's not consistent.
D) Through a clear system — my content, my reputation, and my network work together and bring people to me.

---

**Question 5: What's your biggest challenge with visibility right now?**

A) I don't even know where to start — there are too many platforms, too many strategies.
B) I start things and lose momentum — I've tried a lot but nothing has really stuck.
C) I know what to do, but I'm not doing it consistently enough to get traction.
D) I'm visible, but I want to break through to a bigger stage or a new market.

---

**Question 6: When you think about putting yourself out there — writing, speaking, posting — how do you feel?**

A) Honestly uncomfortable. I'd rather let my work speak for itself.
B) I want to do it, but I overthink it and end up not posting at all.
C) I do it, but I second-guess whether it's good enough or landing the way I intend.
D) Confident. I know my voice, I know my message, and I know it resonates.

---

**Question 7: How clear are you on who your ideal audience is and what they need to hear from you?**

A) Not very clear — I feel like I could help a lot of people, and I'm not sure how to narrow it down.
B) I have a sense of who they are, but I'm not sure my message is speaking directly to them.
C) I know my audience well, but I'm still refining how I communicate my differentiation.
D) Crystal clear — I know exactly who I'm talking to and what they need to hear, and I say it.

---

**Question 8: What would becoming more visible actually change for you?**

A) Everything. Right now I feel like my impact is invisible and my income reflects that.
B) A lot — I know there are opportunities I'm missing because people don't know what I can do.
C) It would accelerate what I'm already building — I'm close, I just need to break through.
D) It would let me play bigger — a larger stage, a wider reach, a bigger mission.

## Scoring Key

| Total Points | Archetype |
|---|---|
| 8–14 | **The Hidden Expert** |
| 15–20 | **The Scattered Voice** |
| 21–27 | **The Rising Authority** |
| 28–32 | **The Magnetic Leader** |

---

## Archetype Profiles (used in the report)

### The Hidden Expert (8–14 points)
Your expertise is real — and largely invisible. You're doing excellent work, but you're operating on the wrong side of a glass wall: the people who need you most can't find you, and you're not sure how to change that without feeling salesy or self-promotional. The good news? You don't need more credentials. You need a visibility strategy that feels like you — one that positions your expertise so the right people can find you and immediately understand why you're the one they need.

### The Scattered Voice (15–20 points)
You know you need to be more visible and you've made real attempts — but the results feel random and the effort doesn't compound the way you hoped. You're active in bursts, but there's no throughline holding it together. The challenge isn't effort; it's that without a clear positioning strategy and a consistent message, every post and every pitch starts from zero. The fix isn't more content — it's a clarity-first approach that makes everything you do work harder.

### The Rising Authority (21–27 points)
You're building real momentum. Your expertise is recognized, your message is getting sharper, and opportunities are starting to find you. The gap between where you are and where you want to be is smaller than it feels — but it matters. What you're missing is the strategic amplification to turn what you're already doing into a consistent, compounding presence that earns you the visibility your work deserves.

### The Magnetic Leader (28–32 points)
You've built something rare — a clear voice, a strong reputation, and a presence that works for you. People seek you out. Your network generates opportunities. You're recognized in your space. The next move isn't to do more — it's to go bigger. New stages, new markets, a wider mission. The question now is: what does your visibility look like at the next level of impact?
