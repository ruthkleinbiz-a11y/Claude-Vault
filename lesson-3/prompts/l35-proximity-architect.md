3. Upload your Universal Expander output

1. Start a NEW conversation in ChatGPT (don't continue the same one)

   1. You can also use the deep research function on any other LLM
   3. Paste your research prompt from Step 1
   4. Let Deep Research work its magic (this may take 2-3 minutes)
