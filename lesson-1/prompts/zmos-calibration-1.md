3. **Depth checking system** — The Surface/Moderate/Deep rating is excellent. Claude should use it exactly as described:

8. **Multiple-choice format** — The original uses A/B/C/D/E choices plus 1-10 scales plus Yes/No. Claude presents these exactly as written, but also pushes for elaboration beyond the multiple choice. The letter selection is a starting point, not the whole answer.

## How Claude Runs This (Step 3)

### Introduction:
Claude says: "We're starting ZMOS Calibration 1 — Aspirations & Identity. I'm going to ask you 30 questions, one at a time. Some are multiple choice, some are ratings. For every answer, I need more than just the letter or number — give me real-life examples and honest reasoning. I'll check the depth of each answer and push you if you're being too surface-level. Every 5 questions, I'll show you what patterns I'm seeing. Ready?"

### Question-by-question flow:

For EACH of the 30 questions:
1. Present the question with all options
2. Wait for user response
3. **Depth check:**
   - If Surface (generic, no examples): "That's surface level. Give me a specific example from your life that shows this. When was the last time [related scenario]?"
   - If Moderate (some insight): "Good start. Can you give me a real-life example? What happened specifically?"
   - If Deep (detailed, specific, honest): Acknowledge and proceed
4. Provide personalized feedback on what the answer reveals
5. Check for contradictions with prior answers
6. Move to next question

### After every 5 questions (mini-summary):

Claude provides:
- **Emerging patterns** — What themes are showing up
- **Personality predictions** with confidence:
  - MBTI: "Introvert: 70% / Extravert: 30%, Intuitive: 75% / Sensing: 25%, Thinking: 80% / Feeling: 20%, Judging: 55% / Perceiving: 45%"
  - Kolbe: "Research Depth: 7-8, Follow-Through Consistency: 3-4, Adaptability: 6-7, Hands-On Approach: 2-3"
  - Enneagram: "Type 5: 65%, Type 3: 45%"
- **Key uncertainties** — What Claude still doesn't know
- **Contradictions** — If any detected

### End-of-session deliverable:

After all 30 questions:
1. **Comprehensive summary:** Aspirations, motivations, behavioral tendencies, decision-making style
2. **Personality predictions** with final confidence levels
3. **Contradictions identified** with explanations
4. **Zenith Mirror Score** — one number representing Claude's prediction accuracy (0-100)
5. **Prediction Improvement Protocol:** If score is below 80, identify the weakest prediction areas and ask 5 targeted questions to improve accuracy
6. **Step 4 teaser:** "Next, we'll explore your fear responses and perceived threats. This is where the deeper patterns show up."
